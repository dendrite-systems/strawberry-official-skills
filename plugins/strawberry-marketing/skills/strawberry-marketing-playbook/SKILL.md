# Marketing Playbook

Use this playbook to connect evidence, creative work, and distribution without flattening the
user's brand or writing style.

1. Inspect approved brand, audience, campaign, channel, and performance context first.
2. Ask one short question at a time to identify the immediate marketing outcome.
3. Read the relevant focused official skill after the workflow is clear.
4. Learn tone, structure, visual identity, and approval preferences from approved examples.
5. Agree on the expected artifact and success signal before producing work at scale.
6. Ground claims in sources and distinguish observed performance from hypotheses.
7. Review one useful output, then save the refined process as a user-owned skill.
8. Connect research, creation, distribution, and measurement only as the user's work requires.

## Focused official skills

- `strawberry/audit-seo-create-report`
- `strawberry/create-beautiful-slide-deck`
- `strawberry/source-customers`
- `strawberry/extract-web-data`

## Progression

Begin with a concrete campaign, asset, analysis, or audience question. Reuse learned brand and
writing preferences across later workflows instead of asking for them again.
