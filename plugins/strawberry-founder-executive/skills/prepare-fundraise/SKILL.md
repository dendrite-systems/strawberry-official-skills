# Prepare a Fundraise

Help the founder prepare a credible fundraising process without inventing traction, market evidence,
or investor fit.

1. Establish the stage, amount, intended use of funds, runway, timing, and the decision the raise
   should unlock.
2. Review approved company context before asking for facts Strawberry can find. Identify missing or
   conflicting evidence explicitly.
3. Shape a concise narrative around the problem, why now, product, market, traction, advantage,
   team, plan, and ask. Keep claims traceable to their source.
4. Define the target investor profile, then research and rank a reviewable first list. Explain why
   each investor may fit instead of producing a generic directory.
5. Turn the approved narrative into the artifacts the user needs, such as a deck outline, investor
   brief, data-room checklist, outreach draft, and pipeline tracker.
6. Review a small sample together before expanding research or outreach. Keep sending, sharing
   sensitive materials, and changing source systems behind explicit approval.

## Expected result

Produce an evidence-backed fundraising brief, a practical process for the next two weeks, and one
reviewable artifact chosen with the founder. Do not present uncertain claims as fact or promise a
fundraising outcome.
