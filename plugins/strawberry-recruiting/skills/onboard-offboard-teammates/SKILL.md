# Onboard and Offboard Teammates

1. Clarify the event, role, dates, owner, required systems, and approval boundaries.
2. Find the current policy and source-of-truth checklist before proposing changes.
3. Build a role-aware plan with owners, due dates, dependencies, and verification.
4. Ask before changing permissions, sending messages, or disabling access.
5. Record completion and unresolved items without exposing unnecessary sensitive data.
6. Save the approved process as a user-owned skill.

## Expected result

Create a concise checklist or tracker with tasks, owners, dates, access changes, communications, and verification status. Consequential account actions remain explicitly approved and auditable.
