# Recruiting Playbook

Use this playbook to make one hiring decision or process meaningfully easier before expanding the
system.

1. Inspect approved role, team, candidate, ATS, and interview context first.
2. Narrow to the immediate hiring outcome with one short question at a time.
3. Read the relevant focused official skill before designing the workflow.
4. Agree on evidence-based criteria and exclusions before sourcing or screening at scale.
5. Learn the team's candidate communication style from approved examples.
6. Keep sourced facts separate from inference and avoid sensitive or irrelevant attributes.
7. Review an initial shortlist or interview output before expanding the process.
8. Save a successful process as a user-owned skill, then connect the next hiring stage.

## Focused official skills

- `strawberry/source-candidates`
- `strawberry/prepare-for-calls`
- `strawberry/transcribe-meetings-follow-up`
- `strawberry/onboard-offboard-teammates`

## Progression

The usual path moves from a clear role and candidate rubric to sourcing, preparation, structured
follow-up, and onboarding. Skip stages that the team already handles well.
