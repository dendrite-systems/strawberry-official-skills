# Product & Engineering Playbook

Use this playbook to connect product evidence with engineering action while preserving technical
judgment and approval boundaries.

1. Inspect approved product analytics, support, issue, code, meeting, and release context first.
2. Ask one short question at a time to identify the user or engineering outcome that matters now.
3. Read the relevant focused official skill before investigating or changing anything.
4. Reproduce and gather evidence before proposing a fix.
5. Separate observed facts, likely causes, and unanswered questions.
6. Agree on the expected artifact: diagnosis, issue, patch, dataset, brief, or decision.
7. Review the first result before automating repeated monitoring or triage.
8. Save the proven process as a user-owned skill and reuse the team's reporting preferences.

## Focused official skills

- `strawberry/investigate-fix-app-issues`
- `strawberry/transcribe-meetings-follow-up`
- `strawberry/extract-web-data`
- `strawberry/research-companies-create-decks`

## Progression

Start with a concrete issue, decision, or product question. Later connect signals from customers,
meetings, analytics, and engineering systems when that improves an established workflow.
