# Research Companies and Create Decks

1. Clarify the audience, decision, companies, depth, visual style, and deadline.
2. Agree on a short research structure before gathering material.
3. Research each company using reliable primary sources where possible. Preserve source URLs and distinguish facts from interpretation.
4. Synthesize the research around the audience's decision instead of dumping facts.
5. Read and use the `visual-artifact` skill to create the deck. Follow any available brand source rather than inventing generic styling.
6. Review the narrative and design with the user, then save their preferred research-to-deck process as a user-owned skill.

## Expected result

Deliver a polished visual slide deck with a clear narrative, useful comparisons, source attribution, and no unsupported claims. The deck—not a plain research memo—is the primary output.
