# Research & Analysis Playbook

Use this playbook to turn a question into defensible evidence and a useful result.

1. Inspect approved existing research, data, terminology, and output preferences first.
2. Ask one short question at a time until the decision, audience, and scope are clear.
3. Read the relevant focused official skill before collecting or transforming information.
4. Agree on sources, fields, inclusion rules, and the expected artifact.
5. Keep source facts, transformations, inference, uncertainty, and missing data distinct.
6. Review a small sample before collecting at scale.
7. Present the conclusion at the depth and in the format the user prefers.
8. Save a successful method as a user-owned skill and reuse its rubric on later research.

## Focused official skills

- `strawberry/extract-web-data`
- `strawberry/research-companies-create-decks`
- `strawberry/create-beautiful-slide-deck`
- `strawberry/student-research-assistant`

## Progression

Begin with a bounded research decision or dataset. Add recurring monitoring or richer presentation
only after the underlying evidence and evaluation method are trustworthy.
