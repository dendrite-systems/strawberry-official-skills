# Save Money Now

1. Clarify the target amount, acceptable tradeoffs, time horizon, and areas that may be reviewed.
2. With permission, inspect relevant subscriptions, invoices, vendors, or recurring expenses.
3. Prioritize credible savings that can be verified and acted on safely.
4. Do not cancel services, change plans, contact vendors, or make purchases without explicit approval.
5. Explain effort, risk, and any downside for each option.

## Expected result

Deliver a short ranked list of realistic savings with evidence, estimated amount, required action, tradeoffs, and the safest immediate next step.
