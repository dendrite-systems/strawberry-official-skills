# Prepare for Calls

1. Confirm which calls to prepare and what success means for each.
2. With permission, inspect the calendar, prior meeting notes, relevant email or chat history, CRM
   records, and public company or person information.
3. Separate known facts from assumptions and surface contradictions or missing context.
4. Prioritize the few details and questions most likely to change the outcome.
5. After the briefs are useful, save the user's preferred preparation format as a user-owned skill.

## Expected result

Produce one concise brief per call with participants, relationship context, purpose, recent
developments, likely concerns, recommended questions, and a clear desired outcome. Optimize for
scanning immediately before the call.
