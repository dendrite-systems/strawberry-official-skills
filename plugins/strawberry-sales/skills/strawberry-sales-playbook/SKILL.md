# Strawberry Sales Playbook

Use this Playbook to find the current sales bottleneck and connect only the Official Skills needed
to remove it.

1. Inspect approved product, customer, CRM, meeting, and communication context first.
2. Ask one short question at a time while deciding whether the immediate need is targeting,
   prospecting, research, outreach, call preparation, follow-up, or pipeline hygiene.
3. Read the relevant focused Official Skill once the first workflow is clear.
4. Agree on qualification criteria and review a small sample before scaling research or outreach.
5. Learn the user's writing style from approved examples before producing messages in volume.
6. Keep facts, inference, sources, and confidence distinct in prospect and account research.
7. Turn a successful first run into a user-owned skill, then connect the next adjacent stage.

## Focused Official Skills

- `source-customers`
- `prepare-for-calls`
- `transcribe-meetings-follow-up`
- `research-companies-create-decks` from Strawberry Research & Analysis

## Progression

A useful progression is qualified prospects, account context, prepared calls, reliable follow-up,
and finally clean pipeline updates. The order should follow the user's real constraint rather than
this list.
