# Transcribe Sales Calls and Follow Up

1. Understand which sales calls matter, who attends, and where notes and follow-ups should live.
2. Confirm the user's preferred note depth and any privacy or retention constraints.
3. Transcribe in the meeting's language and preserve uncertainty where speech is unclear.
4. Extract decisions, open questions, owners, dates, and promised follow-ups.
5. Ask before writing to external systems or sending messages.
6. After the workflow is useful, save its destinations and note structure as a user-owned skill.

## Expected result

Create a readable transcript or linked source plus a concise summary, decisions, and actionable
follow-ups. When approved, update the user's chosen workspace, CRM, or follow-up draft.
