# Venture Capital & Investing Playbook

Use this playbook to connect investment research without turning a preliminary signal into false
certainty.

1. Inspect approved thesis, portfolio, prior memos, evaluation criteria, and writing style first.
2. Ask one short question at a time to identify the decision and evidence needed now.
3. Read the relevant focused official skill before beginning the workflow.
4. Agree on sources, time horizon, exclusions, comparison set, and expected output.
5. Separate sourced facts, calculations, inference, uncertainty, and open diligence questions.
6. Review an initial company, market-map sample, or memo outline before scaling.
7. Preserve the user's memo structure and investment criteria for later work.
8. Save a successful process as a user-owned skill, then connect the next diligence stage.

## Focused official skills

- `strawberry/build-structured-investment-memo-any-company`
- `strawberry/map-startup-ecosystem`
- `strawberry/map-potential-acquirers`
- `strawberry/do-due-diligence-vendor-or-supplier`
- `strawberry/map-key-players-industry-or-ecosystem`

## Progression

Start with the investment question that is actually blocking a decision. Over time, connect
sourcing, market mapping, company research, diligence, and memo creation around the user's thesis.
