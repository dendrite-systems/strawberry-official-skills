# Getting Started with Marketing in Strawberry

Strawberry can work inside the user's analytics, social accounts, CMS, and ad tools, and across the
live web, so research, content, and reporting draw on real data instead of a blank brief. It can
also generate images.

## Setup

- Who the audience is, and what they're selling or promoting right now.
- Which channels matter, and where the numbers live.
- Their voice and brand. Offer to learn it from their website, past posts, and brand files, or let
  them describe it.

## Things to try

- **Learn from what already worked**, e.g. look through their past posts, find the ones that
  performed best, and work out why.
- **See what people are saying about them online**, then turn it into a short report or a deck in
  their brand.
- **See how they compare to competitors right now**. See
  `strawberry/marketing/run-competitor-analysis`.
- **Audit their site's SEO** and get a prioritized list of fixes. See
  `strawberry/marketing/audit-seo-create-report`.
- **Turn one piece of content into posts for each channel**, in their voice, with generated images
  where they help.
- **Plan a campaign** from what's already in their analytics and past launches.
- **Review last month's performance** and decide what to change.
- **Make a beautiful deck or report**. See `strawberry/research-analysis/create-beautiful-slide-deck`.

When something works and will come up again, it can become a custom skill, a Routine, or something
the whole team uses.
