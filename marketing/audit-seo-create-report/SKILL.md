# Audit SEO and Create a Report

Strawberry can work inside Search Console, analytics, and SEO tools the user is logged in to, and
check live search results and pages in the browser, so the audit rests on real data rather than
estimates.

## Setup

- The site, the market, and the pages or products that matter most.
- What the audit is for, e.g. a first look, a traffic drop, a redesign, or a client report.
- Which tools they have, e.g. Search Console, analytics, or a rank tracker, and who reads the
  report.

## Worth knowing

- A short list of prioritized actions beats a dump of every metric. Group related symptoms into the
  underlying problem.
- Don't call something an opportunity on search volume alone. Intent, fit with the product, and how
  hard it is to rank all matter.
- Line up date ranges and markets before comparing periods or tools.
- Screenshots of search results and pages make findings easy to check.
- For client work, keep each client's data and reports separate.
- For a polished report, use the `visual-artifact` skill.
