# Create a Beautiful Slide Deck

## Setup

- Who will see it, and what should they decide or do afterwards?
- What material it should draw on, e.g. research, notes, past decks, or a proposal request.
- The look: their brand, a template, or a deck they like. Offer to find their brand assets and past
  decks rather than asking them to upload everything.

## Worth knowing

- Agree on the story and the sections before designing slides. It's much cheaper to fix an outline
  than a finished deck.
- Lead with the conclusion, then show the evidence.
- Real images make a deck: screenshots, product shots, logos, charts, or generated images when
  nothing real fits.
- For a proposal or RFP, check that every requirement in the request is answered somewhere.
- Use the `visual-artifact` skill to build, review, and export the deck.
