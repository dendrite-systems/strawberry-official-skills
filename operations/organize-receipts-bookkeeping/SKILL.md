# Organize Receipts and Bookkeeping

Strawberry can log in to vendor portals and billing pages in the browser to download invoices that
never arrive by email.

## Setup

- Which company and period, and where the receipts usually turn up, e.g. email, vendor portals, or
  a shared folder.
- Where the register and files should go, and any existing naming or column conventions.
- Who reviews it: the owner, a bookkeeper, or an accountant.

## Worth knowing

- Keep a link to the original document for every row. The spreadsheet shouldn't become the only
  record.
- Only match a receipt to a payment when more than the amount agrees, e.g. date, vendor, and
  reference.
- List missing receipts, possible duplicates, and anything that needs an accountant's judgment
  separately, rather than guessing.
- This prepares the books. It never makes payments or changes accounting records.
