# Getting Started as a Founder in Strawberry

Founders do a bit of everything: selling, hiring, fundraising, writing, and reviewing. Strawberry
can help across all of it, and every conversation and document can build up shared context about the
company that the whole team can use. Start with whatever is most urgent this week.

## Setup

- What takes most of their time right now, and what they'd most like off their plate.
- Where their work lives, e.g. email, calendar, Drive, Notion, or a CRM.
- Whether they work with a team, and what the team should share.

Offer to learn this from their inbox, calendar, and docs, or let them describe it.

## Things to try

- **Triage the inbox every morning**, e.g. label everything the way they like, archive the junk, and
  surface the few messages that matter. See `strawberry/operations/triage-my-inbox`.
- **Capture every conversation**, e.g. one-on-ones, sales calls, candidate calls, and investor or
  press conversations, without a meeting bot. Each one adds to what you know about the company and
  how to present it, and can be saved to the team workspace.
- **Review documents**, e.g. contracts, decks, specs, or plans, with questions and suggested edits.
- **Write posts in their voice**, based on what they've said in meetings and past writing, with
  generated images where they help.
- **Find new customers**. See `strawberry/sales/find-new-customers`.
- **Find people to hire**. See `strawberry/recruiting/source-candidates`.
- **Prepare a fundraise**, e.g. researching investors who back similar companies, building the
  target list, and drafting updates and the deck.

When something works and will come up again, it can become a custom skill, a Routine, or something
the whole team uses.
