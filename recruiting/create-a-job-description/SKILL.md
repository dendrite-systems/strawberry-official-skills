# Create a Job Description

Define the job clearly enough for the hiring team and candidates to make good decisions. The internal role profile and external job description may share facts, but they serve different audiences.

## Workflow

1. Gather available context from workforce plans, role-design notes, approved leveling or compensation frameworks, team documents, existing JDs, scorecards, and relevant legal or policy guidance. Ask only for missing information that materially changes the role.
2. Clarify the business need, outcomes in the first 6–12 months, reporting line, scope, level, location and working pattern, compensation disclosure requirements, essential capabilities, useful experience, and interview evidence.
3. Challenge requirement inflation, vague traits, copied credentials, contradictory constraints, and language that could unnecessarily narrow the pool. Keep the criteria job-related and do not introduce protected characteristics.
4. When internal and external versions are requested, produce them separately:
   - **Internal role profile:** business rationale, outcomes, scope, level, must-haves, acceptable adjacencies, dealbreakers, evidence plan, risks, and unresolved decisions.
   - **External job description:** honest role story, responsibilities, candidate-relevant outcomes, qualifications, location and working pattern, compensation where required or approved, process, and accessibility or accommodation information when supplied.
5. Provide a change summary when revising an existing document. Mark assumptions, policy checks, legal review needs, and unresolved decisions visibly.

## Approval boundaries

The output is a reviewable draft. A qualified human approves the role definition, requirements, level, compensation, and wording. Never publish to a job board or careers page, send it to candidates, or change an ATS record without explicit approval for that exact action and destination.

After approval, use `strawberry/recruiting/map-a-talent-market` when feasibility or search strategy needs testing, `strawberry/recruiting/source-candidates` when the role is sufficiently defined for a shortlist, and `strawberry/recruiting/prepare-candidate-interviews` to turn the accepted criteria into a structured interview plan.

Offer to save the accepted role-profile or JD template as a custom skill when the team will reuse it. Share or schedule only after content, audience, destination, and approval boundaries are agreed.
