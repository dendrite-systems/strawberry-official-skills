# Prepare Candidate Interviews

Design an interview loop that gathers decision-relevant evidence consistently and gives the candidate a fair, coherent experience.

## Workflow

1. Gather the accepted job description or internal role profile, success outcomes, scorecard, candidate materials, prior-stage evidence, calendar context, interviewer availability, process policy, and accommodation information the user is authorized to use.
2. Confirm the decision stage, competencies still to test, interview format, time, interviewers, ownership, and intended output. Avoid repeating questions whose evidence is already sufficient.
3. Build the plan:
   - interview purpose and candidate-facing agenda;
   - competency and evidence ownership by interviewer;
   - structured core questions with job-related probes;
   - scoring anchors and evidence-capture fields;
   - candidate-specific areas to verify, clearly separated from general questions;
   - handoffs, timing, and debrief expectations; and
   - accessibility, privacy, and candidate-experience checks.
4. Prepare separate briefs for the panel and candidate where useful. Limit panel materials to relevant, authorized information. Never introduce protected characteristics, medical details, family status, or unrelated personal information into the evaluation.
5. Check that every must-have has an owner, each interviewer knows what not to duplicate, scoring anchors are usable, and the loop can distinguish evidence from impression.

The output is a draft plan for the hiring team to approve. Do not schedule, send candidate materials, or change ATS records without explicit approval for that action.

After interviews, use `strawberry/operations/transcribe-meetings-follow-up` to create a reliable record when authorized, then `strawberry/recruiting/synthesize-interview-feedback` to map evidence to the accepted scorecard. Offer to save an accepted interview kit as a custom skill; add a Routine only for stable preparation or follow-up steps, never for the hiring decision.
