# Escalate Customer Issue

Give the receiving team enough grounded context to act without making them reconstruct the customer
history. Preserve Support or Customer Success ownership of the customer communication loop.

## 1. Confirm the need and route

Establish the support case or source link, customer-facing owner, current communication state,
impact, timeline, prior attempts, workaround, and why the normal path is insufficient. Choose the
specialist from the actual issue rather than defaulting technical work to one project or owner.

Use breadth, depth, duration, workaround, contractual obligation, and accepted relationship
context to apply the team's priority definitions. Do not inflate priority because an issue is
technical. Follow the accepted urgent path for security, privacy, data loss, legal, regulated, or
active incident cases.

## 2. Build the evidence and ask

Use the complete customer conversation plus relevant account context, product evidence, logs,
screenshots, team discussion, and linked work. Preserve source links, timestamps, versions,
environments, prior attempts, and unknowns. Keep customer data to the minimum the destination needs.

State the exact ask: investigate, reproduce, decide, approve an exception, provide a safe response,
or commit an owner and update time. Distinguish the internal escalation, engineering issue creation,
and customer response as separate actions.

For a product failure, use `strawberry/product-engineering/report-bug` to research, deduplicate,
prepare, file, or update the engineering issue. This skill owns the impact, relationship context,
handoff, and communication obligation.

## 3. Deliver and record the handoff

Provide:

- one-line summary, case or source link, customer-facing owner, and target team;
- customer impact and calibrated priority with evidence;
- timeline, previous attempts, workaround, and current state;
- technical or product evidence and linked work where relevant;
- last customer update and current communication state;
- exact ask, decision deadline, open questions, and next update; and
- brief customer acknowledgement copy for use after the handoff occurs.

Posting internally, assigning an owner, filing or updating an issue, and replying to the customer
are separate external actions. Follow current scoped authorization and verify each completed action.

After the handoff, record the linked owner, named dependency, and next update in the case record.
Send the acknowledgement only after the escalation has happened. Keep the customer-facing owner
responsible until the outcome is verified and the customer loop is complete.
